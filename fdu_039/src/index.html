Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about billing, limits, and integration.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan at any time? +
Yes, AuraStack offers prorated billing so you can switch plans based on your current scaling needs. Changes take effect instantly without downtime.
What happens if I exceed my monthly event limit? +
We will notify you when you reach 80% and 100% of your limit. We offer a soft cutoff, meaning we'll continue ingesting events for up to 3 days while giving you time to upgrade.
Is my data encrypted at rest and in transit? +
Absolutely. All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3, and at rest using AES-256 encryption. We are fully SOC2 Type II and GDPR compliant.
Do you provide an API for custom integrations? +
Yes! Our REST and GraphQL APIs give you full programmetic access to your workspace, allowing you to push/pull data and manage user provisioning securely.
