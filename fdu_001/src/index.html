Async Check-Ins

How It Works

Your Care Journey, Simplified

From onboarding to ongoing insights, CareFlow guides your team through a seamless preventive care experience.

1

Team Onboarding

HR admins import team rosters via CSV or HRIS integration. Each member receives a private invite to complete a baseline health assessment—takes under 8 minutes.

2

Check-In Scheduling

Members choose their preferred cadence—weekly, biweekly, or monthly. Async check-ins sync with Slack and calendar apps. No meetings required.

3

Care Recommendations

CareFlow's clinical engine analyzes responses and surfaces personalized recommendations: specialist referrals, wellness programs, or self-guided resources.

4

Team Insights

Aggregate dashboards help HR leaders identify trends—burnout hotspots, seasonal patterns, and program effectiveness—while preserving individual privacy.

5

Reporting & ROI

Quarterly impact reports quantify outcomes: reduced absenteeism, improved engagement scores, healthcare cost trends, and program participation rates.
