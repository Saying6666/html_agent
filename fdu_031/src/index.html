Frequently Asked Questions

How does billing scale with traffic spikes?
We utilize millisecond-precision billing based on compute time. If your traffic spikes, the nodes auto-scale instantly, and you are only charged for the exact duration those extra resources were active. There are no punitive overage fees.
What frameworks do you support natively?
We natively compile models from PyTorch, TensorFlow, JAX, and ONNX. If you have custom C++ or Rust inference layers, you can deploy them using our secure Docker container registry workflow.
Is data retained after an inference is completed?
No. By default, Nexora operates as a stateless passthrough. Payload data resides in volatile RAM strictly for the duration of the inference and is securely overwritten upon completion. You can opt-in to logging for analytics.
Can I test the Scale plan before committing?
Absolutely. All new accounts come with a 14-day fully-featured trial of the Scale plan. No credit card is required to begin testing our infrastructure.
