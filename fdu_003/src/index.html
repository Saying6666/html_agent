Our Philosophy

Crafted for the Modern Connoisseur

Velvet Leaf was born from a simple belief: that the ancient art of tea deserves contemporary reverence. We traverse remote mountain regions and hidden valleys to source leaves that tell stories of terroir, tradition, and craftsmanship.

Each selection undergoes rigorous cupping sessions by our master tea buyers. Only leaves that meet our exacting standards—complex aroma, layered flavor profiles, and sustainable provenance—earn the Velvet Leaf name.

The Ritual

Brewing as Meditation

We believe that preparing tea is an opportunity to slow down and cultivate presence. Each shipment includes detailed guidance to help you extract the fullest expression from every leaf.

01

Measure with Intention

Use 3-4 grams per cup. Notice the dry leaf aroma—the first whisper of what's to come.

02

Water as Canvas

Each tea has its ideal temperature. Green teas prefer cooler water; oolongs welcome the boil.

03

Time Unfolds

Steep with patience. Watch the leaves dance and unfurl, releasing their essence into the water.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Each month, our tea buyers curate a selection of exceptional teas based on the season and harvest schedules. Your box arrives with detailed tasting notes, brewing parameters, and the story behind each tea. You can modify, pause, or cancel your subscription at any time through your account.

Our Connoisseur and Master tiers allow you to set preferences (green, oolong, black, white, puerh) and exclude any categories you don't enjoy. The element of discovery is central to our philosophy, but we ensure you'll always receive teas aligned with your palate.

We offer complimentary worldwide shipping on all subscriptions. Boxes ship within 3 business days of your billing date. Express shipping is available for an additional fee. All packaging is plastic-free and fully compostable.

Store your tea in a cool, dark place away from strong odors. Our resealable pouches are designed to protect against light and moisture. For long-term storage of aged teas like puerh, we recommend our ceramic storage caddies available in the shop.
