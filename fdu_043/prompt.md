## Round 1: Role + Design System + Sections

# Premium SaaS Landing Page - Modern Glassmorphism & Glo UI

## Project Overview
You are tasked with building a breathtaking, ultra-premium landing page for a cutting-edge SaaS platform using HTML, CSS (Tailwind CSS or custom CSS), and vanilla JavaScript.
This project must exude luxury, innovation, and technological superiority through the meticulous use of glassmorphism, ambient glowing orbs, conic-gradients, and fluid micro-interactions.

## Design Language: Glassmorphism & Glo UI
- **Backgrounds:** Deep, immersive dark themes (e.g., #0B0E14) accented by vibrant, blurred colored orbs
- **Surface Materials:** frosted glass panels using backdrop-filter: blur(20px) 
- **Borders:** Subtle, 1px borders with conic gradients or linear gradients that glow on hover.
- **Typography:** Sleek, modern sans-serif.
- **Motion:** Liquid-smooth transitions, spring physics for hover states, and scroll-linked animations.

## Core Requirements (NO PLACEHOLDERS)
All text, images, and features must be fully fleshed out with realistic, production-ready copywriting tailored to an AI-driven data intelligence platform called Nexus AI.

## Section By Section Breakdown (12+ Sections)

### 1. The Navbar & Hero (Glassy & Atmospheric)
- **Navbar:** Sticky, glassmorphic header.
- **Hero:** 
  - Massive, gradient-text headline: Unlock the Future of Data Intelligence.
  - Background: 3-4 massive blurred orbs moving smoothly via CSS/JS.

### 2. Client Logos (The Trust Layer)
- Title: Empowering industry leaders worldwide.

### 3. Core Features (The Glass Grid)
- A 3-column grid of glassmorphic cards.

### 4. Interactive Dashboard Preview (Tabbed Interface)
- A section showcasing the UI.
- Left side: A list of 4 tabs.
- Right side: A large glass panel displaying the active tab content.

### 5. How It Works (The Timeline)
- A vertical timeline or step-by-step process.

### 6. The Scale of Nexus (Live Metrics)
- Large typography displaying impressive statistics.

### 7. Integrations Hub (The Orbit)
- A central Nexus logo with various tool logos orbiting.

### 8. Use Cases / Solutions
- Clickable cards that expand or flip.

### 9. Customer Testimonials (Masonry Glass Cards)
- 6 detailed quotes from fictional C-level executives.

### 10. Pricing (The Tier System)
- A prominent toggle switch: Monthly / Annually.
- 3 Pricing Cards: Starter, Professional, Enterprise.

### 11. FAQ (The Accordion)
- 5-6 common questions about deployment, data security, and support.

### 12. Explosive CTA (The Grand Finale)
- A dramatic section with intense ambient glowing orbs.

### 13. Comprehensive Footer
- A 4-column layout.
