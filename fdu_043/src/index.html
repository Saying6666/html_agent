Frequently Asked Questions

How secure is my data? ▼
We employ AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit. Your data is isolated in single-tenancy clusters, and we maintain strict SOC2 Type II, GDPR, and HIPAA compliance. We never train public models on your proprietary data.
Do I need a data science team to use Nexus AI? ▼
Not at all. Nexus AI is designed to be accessible for business users through our no-code interface, while offering deep API access and architectural control for advanced data science teams.
How long does integration typically take? ▼
Most of our customers have their first data pipelines and models running within 45 minutes using our native connectors to AWS, Snowflake, Salesforce, and others.
Can I deploy models to my own infrastructure? ▼
Yes. Enterprise customers have the option to export trained models as Docker containers or ONNX files for execution on edge devices or private air-gapped servers.
