FAQ

Common Questions

What prerequisites do I need?

Basic programming experience (Python preferred) and high school-level math. Our prep course gets everyone to the same starting point.

Is the program remote or in-person?

We offer both options. Remote students receive hardware kits by mail and access our labs via telepresence robots.

What payment options are available?

Upfront payment, monthly installments, and income share agreements. Scholarships available for underrepresented groups.

Do you offer job placement support?

Yes, all students receive career coaching, interview prep, and direct introductions to our hiring partner network.
