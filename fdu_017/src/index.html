Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the summit

What time zone will sessions be in?

All sessions are scheduled in Pacific Time (PT) with recordings available within 4 hours. We also provide timezone-optimized schedules for Europe and Asia-Pacific attendees.

Can I get a refund if I can't attend?

Full refunds available until March 1, 2026. After that, tickets can be transferred to another attendee or converted to recording-only access with a 50% credit.

Will recordings be available?

Yes, all sessions are recorded and available within 4 hours. Individual tickets include 30-day access; Team and Enterprise tickets include extended or unlimited access.

What's the difference between live and recording access?

Live attendees can participate in Q&A, breakout discussions, and networking. Recording access includes all content but excludes interactive elements.

Do you offer group discounts?

Yes, teams of 5+ receive automatic 17% discount. Teams of 20+ should contact us for custom enterprise pricing and private workshop options.
